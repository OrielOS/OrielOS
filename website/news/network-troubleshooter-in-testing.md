Two more testing builds are out. They go to computers on the testing channel first, and reach the stable version, the one the [download](/download/) installs, once they've been checked on real PCs. Nothing here is in stable v0.1.9 yet.

## Find and fix network problems

Right click the network icon on the taskbar and choose Find and fix network problems, or search Start for it. It checks each part of the connection in turn: the network adapter and Wi-Fi, the router, name lookups, then the internet. It says in plain words which part is broken and fixes it where it can, such as turning Wi-Fi back on, reconnecting, or opening a hotel or café Wi-Fi's sign in page.

## Rename this PC

Settings → System → About is now OrielOS's own page. It shows the computer's name, its maker and model, and its specifications, with a Copy button for each list. Rename this PC changes the name straight away after an administrator's password, with no restart.

## Date & time

Settings → Time & language → Date & time is OrielOS's own page too, with switches to set the time and the time zone automatically, Sync now, and when the clock last synchronised. Setting the time zone automatically is off until you turn it on. Once it's on, it follows you when you travel.

## GE-Proton, sooner

GE-Proton is now ready as soon as you install Steam. OrielOS sets it up a minute or so after the computer starts, instead of waiting until Steam is there, which could leave Steam without it for a few hours.

## Trying it

Switch to the testing channel in Settings → Update and the newest build arrives by itself. [Release notes](/releases/#v0.3.2) has the full list.

It's still early. [Known limitations](/guides/known-limitations/) lists what doesn't work, including games whose anti-cheat doesn't allow Linux, no Hibernate, and no Microsoft 365 or Adobe apps.
