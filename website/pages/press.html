In one sentence

OrielOS is a free and open-source Linux desktop, tuned for gaming and development and familiar from the first boot.

In a paragraph

OrielOS is a free and open-source Linux desktop for PCs with NVIDIA RTX graphics, tuned for gaming and development and familiar from the first boot. It’s built on Fedora’s image-based system and the KDE Plasma desktop, with a centred taskbar, a Start menu with search, one Settings app and the keyboard shortcuts people know from Windows. Installing apps, updating, changing settings and running games all work without typing a command. The whole system updates as one signed image in the background, and if an update gives you trouble you can go back to the previous version from Settings. OrielOS collects nothing about you or your PC.

Fact sheet

Name
OrielOS (one word, capital O and OS)
What it is
A Linux desktop operating system for PCs
Status
In early development. The first installer, {{release.name}}v0.0.7, came out on 27 October 2026.
Built on
Fedora’s image-based system (bootc), KDE Plasma, and NVIDIA driver modules signed by Universal Blue
Graphics
NVIDIA RTX, 20-series or newer. Checked on real hardware with an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4050 Laptop GPU. AMD and Intel graphics are planned.
Made for
Desktop PCs with UEFI and Secure Boot turned on
Games
Steam and other launchers from the Store, with Windows games running through Proton
Apps
The Store, with apps from Flathub. Firefox is included.
Updates
Signed images, applied on restart, with a way back to the previous version. Stable and testing channels.
Price
Free

No telemetry. This website sets no cookies and runs no trackers.
