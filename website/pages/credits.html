OrielOS stands on the work of many free and open-source projects. Thank you to everyone who makes them.

The projects OrielOS is built on

  • Fedora: the base system and its packages. Licences vary by package, mostly the free licences Fedora lists.
  • bootc: image-based updates, which install OrielOS as one image with a way back to the previous version. Apache-2.0 or MIT.
  • KDE Plasma: the desktop, under OrielOS’s taskbar, Start and look. Mainly GPL-2.0-or-later and LGPL-2.1-or-later.
  • Universal Blue: the NVIDIA driver modules, signed for Secure Boot, and tools for building the image. Apache-2.0.
  • NVIDIA’s driver: the graphics driver for NVIDIA RTX cards. A proprietary user-space driver, with open kernel modules under MIT and GPL-2.0.
  • Flathub: the apps in the Store. Each app has its own licence.
  • Proton: Valve’s compatibility layer, which runs Windows games on Linux. BSD-3-Clause, plus its components’ licences.
  • GE-Proton: a build of Proton with extra fixes, OrielOS’s default for Windows games. BSD-3-Clause, plus its components’ licences.
  • Wine: runs Windows programs, underneath Proton. LGPL-2.1-or-later.
  • DXVK: translates Direct3D 8 to 11 into Vulkan for games. zlib.
  • VKD3D-Proton: translates Direct3D 12 into Vulkan for games. LGPL-2.1-or-later.
  • Firefox: the web browser, there from the first start. MPL-2.0.
