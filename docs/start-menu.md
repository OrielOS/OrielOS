# Using Start

Press the Windows key (Win), or click the Start button in the middle of the taskbar, to open Start. It opens in the middle of the screen, just above the taskbar. Press it again, or Escape, to close it.

![Start, with the search box at the top, pinned apps such as Files, Firefox, the Store and Settings, recommended apps below, and the account and power buttons at the bottom](images/start.webp)

## Search

Start typing as soon as Start opens. The results list apps, settings, files, apps in the Store, and quick calculations such as `12*7`: the best match first, then the rest grouped by kind. The filters above the results (**All**, **Apps**, **Documents**, **Settings** and **Folders**) narrow them to one kind. On the right, the highlighted result shows larger with what you can do with it, such as Open, Pin to Start, Pin to taskbar or the app's own actions. Settings open in [Settings](settings.md), and know Windows' words for them, such as "wifi", "default apps" or "windows update". Typing never opens anything by itself: press Enter to open the highlighted result (the first one, unless you moved with the Up and Down arrow keys), or click a result.

Press **Windows + S**, click the **Search** button next to Start, or click in Start's search box, to search straight away. Before you type, Start shows your **Recent** searches (the ones that opened something) and your **Top apps**, the ones you use most. Click a recent search to run it again; right-click it for **Remove from history**, or click **Clear** to forget them all. Escape goes back to your pinned apps.

## Pinned apps

Your pinned apps fill the top of Start, two rows at a time. **Settings > Personalisation > Start > Layout** gives them three rows (**More pins**) or one (**More recommendations**), and Recommended the rest. With more than two rows, dots beside them show which page you're on: scroll or click a dot to turn the page. To change them, right-click an app anywhere in Start, or select it and press the Menu key or Shift+F10:

- **Pin to Start** adds it to the end of your pinned apps.
- **Unpin from Start** removes it.
- **Move to front** moves a pinned app to the first place.
- **Pin to taskbar** adds it to the apps in the middle of the taskbar, and **Pin to desktop** puts its icon on the desktop.

**Drag** a pinned app to move it to another place. Drop it on another pinned app to make a **folder** of the two, or on a folder to add it there. Click a folder to open it over your pinned apps: click its name to rename it, click an app to open it, or right-click an app and choose **Move out of folder**. Escape or a click beside the folder closes it, and a folder left with one app turns back into that app.

Right-clicking an app also lists the files you opened with it recently, under **Recent Files**, with **Forget Recent Files** to clear them. Point at a recent file and click its pin to keep it at the top of the list, under **Pinned**; pinned files open in that app, and their pin unpins them.

Your pinned apps are saved with your account, so they stay the same after a restart or an update. An app you pinned that isn't installed yet appears as soon as it is.

**All apps**, next to the Pinned heading, lists every installed app in alphabetical order. Click a letter heading to see the alphabet, then click a letter to jump to the apps that start with it. **View** chooses how the apps show: **List** (by letter), **Grid** (icons in rows) or **Category** (a box for each kind of app, such as Games, Internet or Office, with its first apps; click an app to open it, or the box to see all of that kind). **Back** returns to your pinned apps.

## Recommended

Recommended shows apps you installed in the last three days, then files you opened recently. Click one to open it. **More** lists more of them, and **Back** returns. To leave either out, go to **Settings > Personalisation > Start**.

Until you hide them, Recommended also suggests apps to get started with: Steam, Discord, Spotify and OBS Studio. Each one says "Get it from the Store", and clicking it opens its page in the Store, where you choose whether to install it. An app leaves the list once it's installed. To hide the suggestions, right-click one (or press the Menu key on it) and choose **Hide these suggestions**.

## Your account and the power button

At the bottom of Start:

- **Your name** opens Account settings, and lets you Lock the screen, Switch user or Sign out.
- **Downloads**, **Files** and **Settings** open your Downloads folder, your home folder and Settings. **Settings > Personalisation > Start** chooses which of these show next to the power button, and adds Documents, Music, Pictures, Videos, Network or your personal folder.
- **The power button** offers Sleep, Restart and Shut down. When an update is waiting, the button shows an orange dot, and Restart and Shut down say **Update and restart** and **Update and shut down**: either one starts the new version next time. The sign-in screen's power button does the same. While the computer turns off, the screen says **Shutting down** or **Restarting**. There is no Hibernate: see [Games with anti-cheat](anti-cheat.md) for why.

## Using the keyboard

- **Tab** and **Shift+Tab** move between the search field, All apps, your pinned apps, Recommended, your account and the power button.
- **The arrow keys** move within your pinned apps and lists.
- **Enter** or **Space** opens the selected item.
- **Escape** first clears the search, then goes back from All apps, then closes Start.
